System 3 — Supply Chain Multi-Source Synthesis Perturbation

Baseline:
Command:
.venv/bin/supply-chain-investigate meridian --offline

Observed baseline:
- Briefing completed successfully.
- Well-Established section included corroborated and single-source findings.
- on_time_delivery_rate was Contested because supplier_audit reported 95.0% (2026-04-10) while logistics reported 78.0% (2026-04-05).
- production_capacity_utilization was Incomplete because no source reported the metric.

Perturbation:
Command:
.venv/bin/supply-chain-investigate meridian --offline --simulate-timeout

Deliberate change/configuration:
- Simulated a timeout while reading the logistics source.

Observed result:
- The coordinator completed instead of failing.
- logistics was reported as unavailable because of a timeout.
- late_shipment_count was classified as Incomplete with "timeout reading logistics".
- The logistics 78.0% on-time delivery value was unavailable, so on_time_delivery_rate was no longer Contested and the available supplier_audit value of 95.0% remained.
- production_capacity_utilization remained Incomplete because no source reported it.

Reliability observation:
The perturbation demonstrates that the coordinator degrades explicitly when a source becomes unavailable instead of silently treating missing information as confirmed evidence.
